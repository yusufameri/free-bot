36. The fidelity pass is a HARD PIXEL GATE (order 36, user decision 2026-10-18). U6.7 does not stop at geometry measurements: it diffs pixels, app against export, and a delta is a defect until it is fixed or written down. Three rules make the gate real rather than flaky. (a) The app and the export are rendered **in the same browser instance, at the same viewport, in the same run**, and diffed against each other, never app-against-stored-golden-PNG: cross-platform and cross-browser-version rasterization drift is what makes golden-image gates rot, and a same-run pair cancels it. The harness pins the browser build and the fonts the export assumes and says where they came from. (b) Tolerance is near-zero on the shell — sidebar, aside, resizer, header, and the fixed chrome of each screen — and every non-zero pixel in that region is a failure. Content regions that carry real data are diffed per viewport with their data-dependent variance named, so replacing placeholder text with real text is not scored as drift while the layout around it still has to match. (c) The result is reproducible by one command: the nine viewports of the manifest (360x800, 390x844, 430x932, 600x960, 820x1180, 1024x768, 1366x768, 1440x900, 1920x1080), the literal diff statistic per viewport, no horizontal overflow at any of them, and the artifact committed. Exceptions live in `docs/DESIGN-CONTRACT.md`'s delta list with the pixels that differ and the reason the export wins or the delta stands. The diff tool is the unit's own workspace dependency (order 27), added to its own package.json with the lock regenerated.
